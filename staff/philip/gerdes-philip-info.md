
## Philip Gerdes
Philip Gerdes is a Berlin-based software engineer specializing in interactive digital experiences, application development, and creative coding, with an emphasis on web technologies and real-time visualization. His interest in creative coding was sparked during his Communication Design studies at HTW Berlin, from which he graduated with honors. During this time, his interactive installations were exhibited twice at the Ars Electronica Campus exhibition in Linz.

He is currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Creative Technologies at the Film University Babelsberg KONRAD WOLF, where he investigates the intersections of design, technology, and interactive media. His recent work focuses on application development paired with locally deployed machine learning systems, including a knowledge management application and an interactive installation featured at the Sehsüchte Film Festival.
